ADITYA COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY

Aditya College of Engineering and Technology (ACET), located at Surampalem in Andhra Pradesh, is one of the leading engineering institutions in the state. Established under the Sarojini Educational Society, the college is approved by AICTE and affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Kakinada (JNTUK). The campus provides a peaceful learning environment with modern classrooms, advanced laboratories, a well-stocked library, and excellent sports facilities.
